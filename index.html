YouTube → MP3

Save the audio of your uploads as music files, with title, date, artist and cover art.

Include:

Only public videos are listed. For unlisted ones, paste their links in Paste links. For private ones, export your channel with Google Takeout and use the last tab.

You can paste a whole message or list: the links are picked out of the text. Unlisted videos work too, as long as you have their link.

Open the album on YouTube Music and copy its link (Share → Copy link). Each album is saved in its own folder, with numbered tracks, the album's title, artist and year, and its cover. Other YouTube playlists work as albums too.

Settings

Artist, album, track numbers and year come from each album.

If YouTube blocks the downloads

Only needed if songs fail with “Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot”. You must be signed in to YouTube in that browser. Safari also needs Full Disk Access for Terminal (System Settings → Privacy & Security).
