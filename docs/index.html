Your YouTube uploads, as music files.

A free app that saves the audio of your videos as MP3s, with title, date, artist and cover art filled in. It runs on your own computer: Windows, macOS or Linux, in English or Greek, with nothing else to install.

Download

Also for: Windows · macOS (Apple Silicon) · Linux · source code

This app runs on computers. Open this page on your Windows PC, Mac or Linux computer to download it.

Made for downloading your own uploads. Only download videos you have the rights to.

How it works

1

Paste your channel link

The app lists all your videos, live streams and Shorts. Or paste just the links you want. Tick the ones to save, or all of them.

2

Choose the details

In Settings (⚙): MP3, FLAC, WAV or the original audio, your artist and album name, even volume, silence trimmed from the start and end, and where to save the songs.

3

Download

Big channels take a while: the app shows the time left and plays a sound when it's done. Stop any time: songs already saved are skipped when you continue.

What you get

Tagged songs

Title, date, artist, album and square cover art, from your settings or from the song's own details on YouTube Music.

Whole albums

Paste YouTube Music album links: each album gets its own folder, numbered tracks, album tags and its cover.

Private videos too

Convert a Google Takeout export of your channel, including private and unlisted videos.

Original quality

Keep YouTube's own audio without re-encoding, or the audio from your original uploads.

Picks up where it stopped

Saved songs are remembered, so long runs can be split over several days.

Stays on your computer

No accounts, no uploads to a server. The app opens in your browser but runs locally.

The first start

The apps aren't signed with a paid certificate, so your system asks once before opening them.

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Double-click YouTube-MP3.exe.
  3. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open Terminal, type xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine and a space, drag the YouTube-MP3 folder in, press Enter.
  3. Double-click YouTube-MP3.

Linux

  1. Extract the download.
  2. Run ./YouTube-MP3 in that folder.
  3. Optional: install zenity for the folder picker.

A small window stays open while the app works; close it to quit. The app opens in your browser.

Private or unlisted videos? Use Google Takeout

The YouTube tab only sees public videos. Google Takeout gives you every video you uploaded, in the quality you uploaded it:

  1. On takeout.google.com, press Deselect all and tick YouTube and YouTube Music. Under All YouTube data included, keep only videos.
  2. Download the export when Google emails you, and unzip it.
  3. In the app, open Video files, choose the folder and press Convert.
